Acknowledgement:
This game incorporates data derived from JMnedict, the Japanese names dictionary maintained by EDRDG.

Modifications:
Name expressions and readings were extracted from the Yomitan-format data; kana readings were normalized to hiragana; when the reading field was blank, kana values from the glossary field were used; duplicate reading/written-form pairs were removed. JMnedict definition text was not imported into the game's meaning dictionary.
