Changes made for this game
- Replaced the previous SKK-JISYO.L answer dictionary entirely with entries extracted from wty-ja-ja.
- Normalized katakana readings to hiragana and grouped written forms by reading for gameplay.
- Extracted up to three concise Japanese definitions per reading and written form from Yomitan structured content.
- Omitted entries without a usable kana reading, since the game accepts hiragana readings.
- Kept the original Wiktionary page link available in the results screen.
