Changes made for this game:
- Kept Japanese glosses for headwords/readings that match the game's existing SKK-based answer dictionary.
- Converted Yomitan structured definitions to compact plain-text summaries.
- Limited the number and length of definitions shown per word.
- Grouped definitions by normalized hiragana reading and written form.
